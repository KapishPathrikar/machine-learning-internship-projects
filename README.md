## 📂 Projects Included

### 1. [Customer Churn Prediction](Customer%20Churn%20Prediction/)
- **Domain**: Business Analytics & Telecom Retention
- **Dataset**: Telco Customer Churn (7,043 rows, 21 attributes)
- **Objective**: Identify subscribers at risk of canceling their subscription to enable proactive retention campaigns.
- **Key Techniques**: Data cleaning, median imputation, label encoding, class downsampling, hyperparameter tuning (`GridSearchCV`), and model comparison (Logistic Regression, Random Forest, Gradient Boosting).
- **Files**:
  - Notebook: [`Customer_Churn_Prediction_using_ML.ipynb`](Customer%20Churn%20Prediction/Customer_Churn_Prediction_using_ML.ipynb)
  - Dataset: [`WA_Fn-UseC_-Telco-Customer-Churn.csv`](Customer%20Churn%20Prediction/WA_Fn-UseC_-Telco-Customer-Churn.csv)
  - Detailed Docs: [`Customer Churn README.md`](Customer%20Churn%20Prediction/README.md)

### 2. [Heart Disease Prediction](Heart%20Disease%20Prediction/)
- **Domain**: Healthcare & Clinical Diagnostics
- **Dataset**: Cleveland Heart Disease Database (303 patient records, 14 clinical attributes)
- **Objective**: Predict presence of cardiovascular disease from non-invasive diagnostic health metrics.
- **Key Techniques**: StandardScaler pipelines, chest pain analysis, hyperparameter tuning, class balancing, overfitting mitigation, stratified 5-fold cross validation, and single-patient predictive system.
- **Files**:
  - Notebook: [`Heart_Disease_Prediction.ipynb`](Heart%20Disease%20Prediction/Heart_Disease_Prediction.ipynb)
  - Dataset: [`heart_disease_data.csv`](Heart%20Disease%20Prediction/heart_disease_data.csv)
  - Detailed Docs: [`Heart Disease README.md`](Heart%20Disease%20Prediction/README.md)
